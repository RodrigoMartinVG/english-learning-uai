  [ 3. THE FOUR LAWS ]
  • ZEROTH : A ~ C  and  B ~ C   -->   A ~ B
             ~ = thermal equilibrium = no net flow of heat
  • FIRST  : energy is never created or destroyed, only transferred
  • SECOND : in an isolated system, ENTROPY (idea 2) tends to increase
             --> it reaches a MAXIMUM at equilibrium
  • THIRD  : as T -> absolute zero (-273.15 °C),
             entropy -> a constant minimum
