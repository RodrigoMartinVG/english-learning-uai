  [ 3. THE FOUR LAWS ]
  +--------+-----------------------------------+----------------------------+
  | LAW    | THE TEXT                          | OUR CUP                    |
  +--------+-----------------------------------+----------------------------+
  | ZEROTH | A ~ C and B ~ C  -->  A ~ B       | thermometer: tea = room    |
  |        | (~ : no net heat flow)            | --> the tea stops cooling  |
  +--------+-----------------------------------+----------------------------+
  | FIRST  | energy is never created           | the heat is not lost:      |
  |        | or destroyed                      | it goes into the room      |
  +--------+-----------------------------------+----------------------------+
  | SECOND | isolated system: entropy grows    | cup + room together        |
  |        | --> MAXIMUM at equilibrium        | --> never hotter           |
  +--------+-----------------------------------+----------------------------+
  | THIRD  | T -> absolute zero (the lowest)   |                            |
  |        | --> entropy: constant minimum     |                            |
